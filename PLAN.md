# PLAN

How the milestones of SPEC §13 get built. SPEC.md says what; FORMAT.md says the bytes; this
file only orders the work. Status: approved 2026-11-08; milestones 2, 3, 4 and 5 closed (3 on
2026-11-10, 4 on 2026-11-11, 5 on 2026-11-13).

## Milestone 2: toy and synthetic

**Goal:** the whole pipeline, `blobc build` → `blobc verify --tier tiny`, runs on synthetic
inputs. The inputs are real `.osm.pbf` files and Overture parquet, generated by the
`fixtures` module, so nothing in the pipeline is test-only.

### Modules, in build order

Two crates (SPEC §10): `blobmap-model` holds `format` and `runtime`; `blobc` holds every
other module and builds the one `blobc` binary. Each module gets unit tests as it lands. "Old" is `~/Work/blobmap/blobmap-compiler`;
"research" is `~/Work/blobmap/the-new-blobmap-model`.

1. **`format`**: the FORMAT.md writer and reader.
   - **Ports:** old `format/io.rs`, the deterministic safetensors writer and validating
     reader.
   - **Rewrites:** old `model.rs`: manifest and index types, hashes (FORMAT §9),
     validation (§10).
   - **New:** tensors ordered by element size so they are aligned; chunking along
     dimension 0; filling shards unit by unit; `inputs_hash`; ignoring unknown manifest
     keys.
2. **`fixtures`**: synthetic inputs (all new).
   - A minimal `.osm.pbf` writer: protobuf and zlib by hand, checked by reading the files
     back with `osmpbf`.
   - A writer for Overture `address`/`place` parquet and taxonomy CSVs.
   - **Scenarios:** the toy town; islands; one-way traps; a footbridge that joins two parts
     for walkers only; routes just over and just under the cap; equal-time ties; via-way
     `no_*`/`only_*` with one and two via ways; same-piece pairs (one-way and two-way,
     equal offsets); aliases and filtered places; a graph that a small budget forces into
     cells.
   - **Defined early** so every later module tests on real files.
3. **`profile`**: profiles and place rules as versioned TOML, with their hashes.
   - **Ports:** old `profile` (the OSRM v6 car rules, the ACTIVE values of OSRM's
     bicycle/foot).
   - **New:** the place-rules file, and the eligibility rules in the profile (SPEC §6).
4. **`osm`**: reading the `.pbf`.
   - **Ports:** old `osm` (ways, pieces split at intersections, lengths, obstacles and
     their directions), and the restriction parsing of old `edge/edge.rs`.
   - **New:** keeping via-way restrictions (one or more via ways) instead of skipping
     them.
5. **`graph`**: the mode-family graphs and their components.
   - **Ports:** old `graph` and `edge` (CAR turn graph, OSRM turn costs, u-turns,
     node-via restrictions), the ACTIVE union graph, SCC.
   - **New:**
     - **Via-way copies:** old `edge/restrict.rs` (Aho–Corasick states) is ported, and
       gets `only_*` rewriting, two or more via ways, and copy rows (`kind = 2`,
       `copy_of`, `copies`).
     - **Per-way rounding** (SPEC §7) on the stored `f32` times.
     - **Trapped detection:** see the reading under "Open items".
     - **Component numbering** (FORMAT §4).
6. **`order`**: the node order and cells.
   - **Ports:** RoutingKit inertial-flow nested dissection, vendored with its OpenMP
     patch, whose order is bit-identical across thread counts (old `order`).
   - **New:**
     - an `Orderer` trait, with nested dissection and a fixed order for the toy town;
     - cells: order the fine graph as in one piece, cut the elimination tree of that
       order where subtrees fit `memory_budget_bytes`, keep the order in every block
       (SPEC §5). Fallback when the fine graph is too large to order: coarsen, take the top
       separators from the coarse ordering, lift them to the fine graph so that no fine arc
       joins two cells; each cell and the top block are then ordered on their own.
7. **`cch`**: the hierarchy.
   - **Ports:** old `contract` (chordal completion, elimination tree, levels),
     `customize`, `layout`.
   - **New:**
     - blocks (a top block then cells, level order inside a block);
     - packs and link references across packs (FORMAT §4);
     - customizing blocks in order (cells before the top);
     - deriving triangles;
     - the 2³² pack limits.
8. **`overture`**: the Overture inputs.
   - **Ports:** old `overture` (parquet reading, taxonomy, redirects, confidence and closed
     filters, the 30 m dedup) and old `geo/region.rs` (`.poly` containment) for
     `--extent`.
   - **New:** aliases for merged places; `FilteredPlace`; `NONE16` for keys missing from
     the taxonomy; the UUID check that fails the build; the two extent modes (SPEC §3).
9. **`snap`**: IDs onto roads.
   - **Ports:** old `snap` (local tangent plane, grid index, nearest piece, foot of the
     perpendicular, at-node rule).
   - **New:** snapping per mode for bike and walk; skipping trapped pieces and copies;
     offsets by the rounding rule (SPEC §7); the four reason codes.
10. **`build`**: the stages behind a `Stage` trait.
    - **Rewrites:** old `pipeline`.
    - **New:**
      - packs shared by small components;
      - the model writer (packs, shards, manifest, `LICENSES`);
      - the data report (per-country and per-category counts, trapped components, `NONE16`
        places);
      - the build report (wall time and peak RSS per stage, largest pack per family);
      - both reports written outside the model directory.
11. **`runtime`**: the CPU reference runtime.
    - **Ports:** old `refruntime` (sweeps, multi-source columns, bounded sweeps, seeds,
      `EdgeDijkstra`, re-customization).
    - **New:**
      - car `to` heads through the exit turns;
      - arrivals through copies;
      - the direct term;
      - aliases and reasons;
      - every operation of SPEC §9, `multi_criteria` included.
12. **`verify`**: the conformance suite: the tiers, the checks of SPEC §11, golden files (FORMAT §11), and
    the mutants.
    - **Ports:** old `conformance`.
    - **New:** the turn-aware Dijkstra that tracks the segments driven, independent of the
      copy construction; the via-way, cells, cap, ties and car-`to` cases.
    - **Memory:** the checks on the model's bytes run first, with no reference; above the
      tiny tier the reference is then built and checked one family at a time; each check
      holds one of the model's components at a time; the golden replay holds no reference.
13. **`cli`** (`blobc`'s `main.rs`): `blobc build | verify | inspect`, thin over `build`,
    `verify` and `blobmap-model`, and the tools (`fixtures`, `extract`, `tier`,
    `accuracy`, `components`, `fragments`) as subcommands with their own arguments.

### Gate check

The gate passes on a clean commit when all of these hold:

1. **Unit tests:** `cargo test --workspace` passes, with zero warnings under
   `cargo clippy`.
2. **Tiny tier:** for every fixture scenario, `blobc build` then `blobc verify --tier
   tiny` shows zero mismatches on every check of SPEC §11 that applies, and golden files
   are written and replayed. Specifically:
   - the toy town gives its 17 links, 5 levels and distance grid;
   - every mutant fails.
3. **Determinism:** `tools/determinism.sh` builds every scenario with 1, 2, 8 and 24
   threads (`RAYON_NUM_THREADS` and `OMP_NUM_THREADS`). The SHA-256 of every file in the
   model directory must be equal across runs.
4. **Cells:** the cells scenario built with a small budget and in one piece gives
   different `model_id`s but the same `inputs_hash`, and replays the same golden file.

`ci.sh` runs 1–4 locally (SPEC §11: every commit).

Decided at approval: trapped components follow SPEC §5 ("Precisely"), and the data report
compares against `--previous-report` (SPEC §3, §10).

## Milestones 3–6

Derived data (city and region extracts, the neighbours `.pbf`) goes in a new directory
outside the read-only reference folders.

- **3. France.**
  - **Build:** the France `.pbf` with `--extent france.poly`. Peak RSS ≤ 62 GB, read from
    the build report.
  - **Gate:**
    - small tier (a Lille extract), region tier (HdF) and country tier all green;
    - accuracy against local OSRM data, on the research's cached pairs, never the live
      API:
      - car: MAE ≤ the research engine's (about 19 s). The report notes that via-way
        restrictions are now applied, unlike in the research, so the two engines differ.
      - walk: median relative error about 0.06 %, as in the research.
      - bike: median relative error about 5 %, as in the research (the missing turn costs,
        SPEC §4);
      - the pairs answered `DifferentComponent` are only those a real island or a ferry
        separates;
    - no ID unserved because of its component: the IDs per reason code, Corsica's and the
      islands' served counts, and the largest components that serve no ID.
- **4. Any pbf.**
  - **Extracts** (Geofabrik, with their `.poly` as the extent), each built, then verified
    with the small-tier checks and samples, the via-way copy check exhaustive (every
    prefix), and golden files written and replayed. Their tier file is `tiers/any/small.toml`
    (`--tiers tiers/any`), apart from the small tier that CI builds on every commit.
    - Great Britain and Japan: left-hand traffic. Turn penalties follow the driving side as
      in OSRM v6 (`turn_bias` inverted where traffic keeps left).
    - Fiji: the 180° meridian (extent, snapping grid, island polygons, ordering), and roads
      joined at the twin nodes OSM leaves on either side of it (SPEC §4): Taveuni's road is
      one component. The planet input pass lists every twin.
    - Norway: ferries excluded, so fjord crossings split components; the data report and
      `blobc components` show which.
    - India: sparse tagging (untagged speeds, missing one-ways, unconnected ways).
    - California: a large US state.
  - **Planet, input stages only:** the planet `.pbf` and all of Overture, with node
    locations in a disk-backed, memory-mapped index; peak RSS per stage in the build
    report.
  - **Gate:**
    - every extract at zero mismatches, its data report reviewed (components, islands
      emptied, reason codes, `NONE16` places);
    - the planet input stages within 62 GB.
- **5. Continent.**
  - **Fallback:** Hauts-de-France, France, and one `.pbf` covering France, Belgium,
    Luxembourg, Switzerland and Germany, each with the coarsening fallback forced, and
    each in one piece.
  - **Work, in order, with HdF numbers for each:** flow-based refinement of the lifted
    coarse separators on a fine band around each separator; then, for CAR, an order from
    the node-based intersection graph lifted to the turn graph. Keep whichever reaches
    ≤ 1.25× sweep layers with the lower peak RSS.
  - **Verify split per component on disk,** so that it scales past 1.5× France.
  - **Europe:** built and verified.
  - **Gate:**
    - the fallback: zero mismatches at every tier, cross-border golden checks green; sweep
      layers (FORMAT's level count) ≤ 1.25× one piece, links and elimination-tree height
      reported beside them in the build report; cells cut from the fine order give links
      and elimination-tree height equal to one piece; peak RSS ≤ 62 GB;
    - Europe built within 62 GB and verified; the build report gives bytes per link, per
      address and per column, the largest pack per family (with Eurasia's top block below
      2³² in every count), and the extrapolation to the world.
- **6. World.**
  - **Gate:**
    - the planet built within 62 GB;
    - verified at the tiers that apply;
    - sizes reported (components per continent from the component tensor, bytes per part)
      for choosing the serving hardware.
